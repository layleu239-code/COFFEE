Our story

Made slowly.
Shared gladly.

Crumb & Pour is a neighborhood cake studio and coffee bar built around the good things: honest ingredients, kind service, and a fresh slice at the right time.

Since 2018

One oven.
A lot of heart.

We started with a hand-me-down mixer and a recipe book full of notes in the margins. Our kitchen has grown, but our way of working has stayed small and thoughtful.

Every cake is finished by hand. Every drink is made to order. We believe the best hospitality is the kind that makes an ordinary afternoon feel worth remembering.
