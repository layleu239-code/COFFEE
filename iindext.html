Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Can I order a whole cake?

Absolutely. Whole cakes serve 6–12 people and can be reserved by email with 48 hours notice.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. We deliver around Phnom Penh every day. Delivery is arranged when we confirm your order.

Do you have dairy-free options?

Several drinks can be made dairy-free with oat or coconut milk. Please ask us about cake ingredients before ordering.
